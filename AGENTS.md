## Purpose

VendoTube Studio — an Electron desktop app in which a crew of AI agents (Showrunner, Scout, Director, Producer) produces evidence-led, rights-safe YouTube documentaries end-to-end: visible-browser research, Documentary Bible scenario design, neural TTS narration (edge-tts), HyperFrames/GSAP motion-graphics rendering, QC, and delivery. It supersedes the v1 WPF chatbot product plan; the DWK CLI toolkit itself remains the production engine, reused unchanged.
